About Service

We make the daily journey of a homemade lunch easier for families.

A dedicated delivery service

Homemade lunch, made easier for busy days.

AVR DABBA is Hyderabad's first specialized home-to-school lunch box delivery service, designed for busy parents, working professionals and students who find daily lunch delivery challenging.

Prepare food at a convenient morning pace, pack the lunch box, and hand it over to our verified delivery partners. We focus on lunch-box transport—we do not cook or prepare food.

Routes, handover points and timing are agreed with each family before service starts. For office or campus deliveries, contact us to discuss the destination and available route.

What Is Our Service?

We pick up ready-to-go lunch boxes from an agreed home pickup point and deliver them to the agreed school handover point in Hyderabad.

Pickup is typically planned between 9:30 AM and 11:30 AM, depending on school distance and the route. We confirm the actual pickup window and school arrangements with each family.

How It Works — Daily Operations Routine

Each step is arranged to support careful transport and a smooth handover to school.

  1. 01

    HOME

    Choose Your Plan & Book

    Select a suitable delivery plan and contact us through WhatsApp or the website to discuss your route and booking.

  2. 02

    PICKUP

    Cook Fresh & Get Doorstep Pickup

    Prepare and pack your child's homemade lunch. Pickup is arranged with your family, typically between 9:30 AM and 11:30 AM depending on the route.

  3. 03

    SAFE TRANSIT

    Careful, Insulated Transit

    Where routes allow, packed lunch boxes travel in insulated bags to help protect them during delivery.

  4. 04

    SCHOOL

    School Drop-off & Handover

    We deliver to the agreed school handover point, subject to route availability and school access.

  5. 05

    LUNCHTIME

    Homemade Meal at School

    Your child can enjoy the homemade meal you prepared at school according to the confirmed delivery arrangement.
