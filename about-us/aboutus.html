Our story

We Saw the Daily School-Lunch Gap

Every parent wants their child to have a lunch prepared with care at home. But for working parents and busy families, getting that packed lunch from home to school can be a challenge.

That's where AVR DABBA began.

We realised that parents didn't need another food service. They needed a simple way to get the food they had already prepared to their child at school.

Parents prepare and pack the lunch at home. We handle the pickup and delivery, helping make the journey from the parent's kitchen to the child's school easier.

Frequently Asked Questions

Anything else you'd like to know? Call or WhatsApp us on 92812 71759.

Do you cook the food?

No. Parents prepare and pack the homemade meal. AVR DABBA provides the pickup and delivery service.

What exactly does AVR DABBA do?

We arrange to collect a child's packed lunch from the agreed pickup point and deliver it to the agreed school handover point, subject to route and school access.

Do you provide the lunch box?

Please pack the meal in your child's own lunch box, ready for the agreed pickup.

Where do you operate?

We coordinate service in Hyderabad. Routes and school access vary, so contact us with your area and school to check availability.

What time is pickup?

Pickup is planned around the route. We confirm the available pickup time and school handover arrangements with each family before service begins.

How can I book?

Call or WhatsApp us with your child's school and area. We'll discuss route availability and the next steps with you.
